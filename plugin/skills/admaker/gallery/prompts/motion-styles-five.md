## Prompt

```text
A 21-second 9:16 reel that shows five motion-graphic styles over six footage clips, 3.5 seconds each, with everything transparent over the footage. 1) Kinetic type: "SEE IT", "LOVE IT", "OWN IT" slam in one per beat at 120 BPM, a full-width brand-blue bar wipes in behind "LOVE IT", earlier lines punch on each new beat, and on the next beat all lines squash and spring back. 2) Squash and stretch: three blue balls drop in with phase offsets, flatten on contact, stretch as they rise, cast shrinking shadows, and settle with a wobble before "LOADING" bounces in. 3) Flat isometric: a pastel block city builds from back to front, each block rising from the bottom with a small landing squash. 4) The same city in real 3D with an orthographic camera that slowly orbits 40 degrees after it builds. 5) Liquid: glossy blue metaballs swell in, drift and merge, with "STAY CURIOUS" below. 6) Particle logo: scattered twinkling dots swirl together into the word "CREATE". No dim or haze over the footage.
```
